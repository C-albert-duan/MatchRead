# Module: i18n (`packages/i18n`)

**Package:** `@matchread/i18n`  
**Role:** Locale catalog and helpers for product copy.

## Surface

- Locales: `en` | `es` | `ja`
- Entry: `packages/i18n/src/index.ts`
- Wired in the web app via `apps/web/lib/i18n.ts` and shell `LocaleSwitcher`

## Boundaries

- Strings and locale selection only — no domain math, no data fetching.
- Keep tournament/player **facts** (names from provider) out of translation; translate product chrome and status captions.
